Questions

A few practical details.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on the scope, content, integrations, and review schedule. A timeline is agreed after we understand the requirements.

How is pricing decided?

Pricing is based on the agreed deliverables and complexity. You receive a defined scope and price before development begins.

Who pays for the domain and hosting?

Domain, hosting, and third-party service costs depend on your chosen setup. Any required recurring costs should be identified before launch.

Can you provide ongoing support?

Support and maintenance can be discussed and scoped based on the website or application after launch.
